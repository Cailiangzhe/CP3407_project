# Initial Backlog Ideas

This is the initial product backlog for FreshTrack. The stories below are rough and will be refined, split, and prioritised as the project progresses.

Each story uses the format:

> As a **[user]**, I want **[goal]**, so that **[benefit]**.

---

## User Stories

1. **User registration** — As a new user, I want to register for an account with a username and password, so that my pantry data is kept private and separate from other users.

2. **User login** — As a returning user, I want to log in with my credentials, so that I can access my own pantry inventory securely.

3. **Add food item** — As a user, I want to add a new food item with its name, category, quantity, purchase date, expiry date, and estimated price, so that the system has an accurate record of what I have at home.

4. **View pantry inventory** — As a user, I want to view all of the food items currently in my pantry, so that I can see at a glance what is available before I cook or shop.

5. **Edit or delete food item** — As a user, I want to update the quantity of a food item or delete it from my inventory, so that the pantry list stays accurate when food is partly used or thrown out.

6. **Expiry risk reminder** — As a user, I want the application to clearly show me which items are close to expiring, so that I can use them before they go to waste.

7. **Recipe recommendation** — As a user, I want the system to suggest simple recipes based on the ingredients I already have, so that I can use food that is close to expiry instead of buying more.

8. **Shopping list generation** — As a user, I want to build a shopping list that includes both items I add manually and items suggested by the system, so that I do not forget what I need next time I go to the shops.

9. **Waste log** — As a user, I want to record whether a food item was eaten, expired, discarded, or donated, so that I can see how my food is actually being used over time.

10. **Dashboard analytics** — As a user, I want to see a dashboard showing my near-expiry items, monthly waste quantity, and estimated money lost, so that I can understand my food habits and try to improve them.
